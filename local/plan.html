Important risks before investing

1. Banking sector

Check classified loans, rescheduled loans, provisions and capital adequacy. Rising profit alone is not enough.

2. Corporate governance

Check sponsor and director holdings, related-party transactions and auditor qualifications.

3. Liquidity

Some shares are hard to sell at a fair price.

4. Dividend traps

A high dividend yield does not guarantee future dividends.

5. Price manipulation

Do not buy on Facebook groups, rumours or broker tips.

6. Diversification

Do not put your emergency savings in shares.

Keep your emergency fund separate. A 3–5 year horizon is short for shares. Do not invest money that you must take out on a fixed date.

How to start investing in Bangladesh

  1. Open a BO account through a licensed DSE broker or authorised participant.
  2. Choose a brokerage firm. Compare fees, support, online trading and withdrawals.
  3. Deposit a small amount that you can leave invested.
  4. Research before you buy: latest financial statements, disclosures and valuation.
  5. Keep an investment journal: price, quantity, reason, holding period and risks.

Next research phase

For each company: five years of audited financials, quarterly results, dividends, price history, valuation, ownership and disclosures. Compare companies inside the same industry. A bank's P/E is not comparable to a pharma company's P/E.
